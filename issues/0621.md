## Purpose

Evaluate maintained decentralized and confidential-compute systems that may address existing SDK gaps. This issue is a research intake and routing record. It does not approve a system, assign a trust tier, change policy, or mark a production-readiness issue mitigated.

## Candidate domains

- Confidential compute and hardware attestation: Phala dStack, Oasis Sapphire, Secret Network, AWS Nitro.
- Threshold authorization and key services: Lit Network and maintained threshold implementations.
- Data availability and storage: Celestia, Avail, Arweave, Filecoin.
- Signed data and oracles: Pyth, Chainlink.
- Bitcoin-compatible watchtowers and operator networks.
- Interoperability: IBC, Hyperlane, LayerZero, Wormhole and existing approved bridge contracts.
- Verifiable compute: Ritual, Akash and maintained proof/attestation runtimes.
- Asset/RWA systems such as Centrifuge and Goldfinch remain outside production scope unless separately approved after legal, custody, data-rights, risk, and value-authority review.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each candidate has source-dated primary references, maintained version/contract, license, governance, and security/trust assumptions.
- [ ] Applicability is mapped to an existing issue (#200, #202, #240, #241, #242, #271 or an existing watchtower owner) without creating a duplicate backlog.
- [ ] A maintained implementation is preferred; any adapter is thin, fail-closed, and covered by compatibility and negative tests.
- [ ] Exact source, artifact, configuration, deployment, runtime, recovery, rollback, and evidence times are recorded where implementation is proposed.
- [ ] Provider claims, cryptographic proofs, availability, custody, and data/control boundaries are independently verified.
- [ ] Applicable OpenSpec, CODEOWNERS, promotion, soak, release, and independent acceptance controls pass before any production use.
- [ ] Business #1390/#1423/#1424 records adoption or rejection and ownership.

## Boundaries

- Preserve the Bitcoin-rooted universal-chain scope and zero-native-token policy.
- Do not build custom consensus, attestation-quorum, oracle, bridge, or storage substitutes without an evidenced gap and accepted design.
- Do not activate staking/restaking, yield, RWA, pricing, fee splitting, treasury, custody, settlement, or any value-bearing operation from this research.
- Documentation, metadata, test fixtures, and collector assertions are not production or Mainnet acceptance.
