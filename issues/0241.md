# [P0] Qualify Android KeyMint/StrongBox authorization and Play Integrity verification

**Issue #241** | OPEN | Created: 2026-07-22 | Updated: 2026-07-25

Parent: [SDK umbrella #195](https://github.com/Conxian/conxius-enclave-sdk/issues/195)

Linear umbrella: [CON-1512](https://linear.app/conxian-labs/issue/CON-1512/p0-enforce-hardware-backed-signing-and-mandatory-attestation-for-value)

This focused tracker does not close #195 or CON-1512 by itself.

## Goal

Real Android device/app authorization proof for the Android-first wallet.

## Boundary

KeyMint/StrongBox and Play Integrity prove device/app/key authorization; they do not by themselves prove Bitcoin/Stacks protocol-key custody. P-256/device authorization stays separate from secp256k1/Schnorr protocol signing.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Capture and verify real-device KeyMint/StrongBox evidence.
- [ ] Verify DER/X.509 chain, trusted root, validity/revocation, nonce, package/app binding, key purpose, security level, verified boot/patch, user authorization, and operation binding.
- [ ] Verify Play Integrity server-side and bind it to the exact request.
- [ ] Make StrongBox vs Android TEE downgrade explicit by policy/tier.
- [ ] Fail closed for unsupported devices, provider outages, software evidence, and fixtures.
- [ ] Add reproducible positive, stale, replayed, revoked, malformed, wrong-app, wrong-key, and unsupported evidence.
- [ ] Prove no private key crosses native/Rust/WASM/JavaScript boundaries.
- [ ] Link exact release, SBOM/provenance, real-device matrix, and independent review evidence.
