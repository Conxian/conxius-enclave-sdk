## Summary

Adds real cryptographic execution for FROST threshold signing, backed by the Zcash Foundation FROST library (frost-secp256k1-tr v3.0.0, RFC 9591, MIT/Apache 2.0).

Gated behind the frost-crypto feature flag. Without the flag, existing boundary validation behavior is unchanged.
