**PR #255** | CLOSED | Author: charliecreates[bot] | Created: 2026-07-26 | Merged: 2026-07-26

Branch: `charlie/191-durable-replay-conformance` → `main`

## Summary
- add a factory-driven conformance suite for the active `ReplayStore` contract, including atomic batches, contention, retention, validation order, clock high-water behavior, and full binding isolation
- add a deterministic test-only fault/recovery model for pre-commit outages, indeterminate post-commit responses, snapshot/restore, and all-or-nothing batch recovery
- document backend requirements and primary-source research without selecting a datastore or changing production-support claims
- add `ReplayBatchOutcome::accepted_for` so external adapters can construct an exact successful batch outcome without supplying an arbitrary count

## Why

This advances the remaining replay gate identified by #191 and #240. It creates executable requirements for a future durable adapter while preserving the current fail-closed boundary: the reference model and `DurableProvider` fixtures are contract evidence only, not provider or deployment evidence.
