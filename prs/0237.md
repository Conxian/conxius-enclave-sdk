## Summary

Phase A of CON-1512 adds a typed, fail-closed proof boundary for value-bearing operations:

- Adds `src/enclave/proof.rs`, exported through `enclave`, with separate subjects (`server`, `user`, `phone/device`) and exact mechanisms (`server identity`, `user authorization`, `phone/device attestation`, `TEE attestation`, `FIDO2/WebAuthn assertion`, and `TPM quote`).
- Separates bounded raw evidence from private-field verified proof types and exact-type verifier dispatch.
- Adds versioned/domain-separated canonical contexts covering proof identity, issuer/trust identity, nonce, operation digest, purpose, policy, subject binding, evidence digest, freshness, and replay identity.
- Adds a versioned/domain-separated canonical digest for the complete `ProofSetPolicy`, including freshness constraints and the exact requirement set; requirement declaration order is canonicalized.
- Binds the expected exact proof policy digest into `ValueBearingSignRequest` and its operation binding, and replaces unchecked proof-set attachment with request-checked attachment.
- Revalidates the exact policy digest at the private value-bearing rail authorization boundary; proof-set propagation includes both policy digest and set digest/count.
- Adds explicit all-required proof-set composition with typed per-proof failures, duplicate/conflict/type-substitution rejection, and deterministic canonical set binding.
- Requires an independently verified proof set at the private value-bearing rail authorization boundary; legacy `DeviceIntegrityReport` evidence is not silently upgraded.
- Keeps production provider verification unavailable and test fixtures unable to satisfy production policy.

## Security invariants

- Missing, unsupported, stale/future, mismatched, duplicate, conflicting, substituted, or incomplete proof sets fail closed.
- Same-policy-ID proof sets with altered requirement keys, issuer, trust root, subject binding, freshness limits, purpose, nonce, operation digest, replay identity, or partial composition cannot be attached.
- Existing software/test providers, `AttestationPolicy::production()`, identity creation, built-in rail dispatch, and distributed-production replay claims remain unchanged and fail closed where previously required.
- Diagnostics redact raw evidence bytes; proof-set rail propagation carries only verified summary binding data.
